Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bleris Cakes and Pastries collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided through this website.

Effective date: 12 July 2026 Owner: Bleris Cakes and Pastries Location: Kumba, Cameroon

1. Information we collect

When you place an order, request a quote, or subscribe to updates, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, and order details. We also use local browser storage for the shopping cart and theme preference.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and confirm orders placed through the website or WhatsApp.
  • To communicate about your order, delivery, or enquiry.
  • To improve website performance, security, and user experience.
  • To send occasional product updates or promotional emails only when you consent.

3. Data sharing and storage

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers that support payment, order handling, or website operations, and we take reasonable steps to protect it.

4. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at info@bleriscakes.com. You may also opt out of marketing emails at any time.

5. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at info@bleriscakes.com or call +237 672 874 246.
