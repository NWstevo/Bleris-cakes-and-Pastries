Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Bleris Cakes and Pastries uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Effective date: 12 July 2026 Updated regularly

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored in your browser that help websites remember information such as your cart contents and visual preferences.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: keep the shopping cart, navigation, and checkout experience functioning.
  • Preference cookies: remember your selected dark/light theme so the site stays consistent.
  • Consent storage: records your cookie preference selection so the banner does not keep reappearing.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the shopping cart and checkout experience.
